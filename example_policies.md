# Example Policies for Testing

Use these sample policies to test PolicyAI:

## 1. Simple Expense Policy
```
Employees must submit expense reports within 30 days of purchase. All expenses over $500 require manager approval. Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action. Receipts must be original and itemized. Travel expenses require pre-approval for trips over 100 miles. Company credit cards are for business use only.
```

## 2. Remote Work Policy
```
Remote employees must be available during core business hours (9 AM - 5 PM local time). All remote workers must have a dedicated, distraction-free workspace. Company-provided equipment must be used for work purposes only. Video must be enabled for all team meetings. Performance will be evaluated quarterly based on deliverables and communication. Internet connection must meet minimum speed requirements.
```

## 3. Social Media Policy
```
Employees should not disclose confidential company information on social media. Official company social media accounts are managed by the marketing department. Personal opinions should include a disclaimer that views are your own. Do not engage in arguments or defamatory comments about the company, colleagues, or competitors. All social media activity reflecting on the company should be professional.
```

## 4. Data Security Policy
```
All company data must be stored on approved cloud services. Personal devices must not store sensitive information. Passwords must be changed every 90 days and should include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters. Report lost devices immediately. Data backups occur nightly. Unauthorized data sharing is prohibited.
```

## 5. Vacation Policy
```
Employees accrue vacation time at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Vacation requests require 2 weeks notice. Maximum consecutive vacation is 2 weeks. Holiday blackout periods apply during December. Unused vacation carries over up to 5 days. Manager approval required for all vacation.
```

## Tips for Testing:
1. Copy any policy text above
2. Paste into the PolicyAI text area
3. Click "Analyze Policy"
4. Review the structured analysis
